Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AICrea ("AICrea", "we", "us") may collect, use, disclose and protect information when you use the AICrea application and related services.

1. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, AICrea may handle information such as:

  • Account information, including your Google sign-in email address and basic profile details.
  • Prompts, conversations, generated responses, notes, saved items, quizzes, projects and other content you choose to keep in AICrea.
  • Files and media you submit for AI generation, transcription, editing, clip analysis or other requested tasks, including temporary processing copies.
  • Connected social-account and channel information, including authorization tokens needed to provide integrations you explicitly connect.
  • Google Play purchase information needed to verify credit packs and subscriptions, such as product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase state, credits granted and subscription expiry. AICrea does not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Support requests and replies, including the email address, subject and message you submit.
  • Operational and security records, such as request timestamps, rate-limit events, provider errors and AI usage metadata (for example provider/model, token counts, audio duration and credits consumed). These records are used to monitor service reliability, abuse and operating costs.
  • Technical and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide and personalize AICrea features, respond to your requests, maintain saved items, process subscriptions or credits, provide support, protect the service against misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. AI processing and service providers

When you use an AI feature, the prompts, text, files or other inputs needed to fulfil your request are sent to the model provider selected for that feature. Depending on the tool, AICrea may use DeepSeek, Groq or OpenAI for AI processing, and Supabase for authentication, backend and database services. Google provides sign-in and Google Play purchase processing; Meta and Google receive information needed for social integrations only when you connect and use those features. AICrea may use Resend or Brevo for support email delivery when configured. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not submit information you do not have permission to share.

4. Connected accounts and publishing

If you choose to connect a third-party account, AICrea may receive authorization tokens and account or channel information needed to provide the integration you requested. If you authorize publishing, the content and details you select may be sent to the relevant platform. You can disconnect integrations where that control is available. Your use of those platforms is also governed by their own policies.

5. Storage and retention

Cloud account data and saved content are retained while your account remains active, unless you delete the relevant item earlier or a shorter retention period applies. Temporary media copies are kept only for the processing and cleanup period needed by the feature. Support tickets and security records are retained only as reasonably necessary to handle requests, prevent abuse, troubleshoot incidents and meet legal obligations. Google Play keeps its own payment and order records under Google's policies. Third-party platforms may retain information under their own policies.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate AICrea, with a platform when you request a connected action, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service.

7. Your choices and requests

You can use the in-app account deletion option or the public Account & Data Deletion page to request deletion. You may also contact us to request access to or correction of your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or ask about data portability. We handle applicable requests under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other laws that apply to you. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it or where it is necessary to resolve disputes, protect the service or establish legal claims. You may also lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children

AICrea is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

10. International processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, depending on the location of our service providers.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy as AICrea changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Support@mexbui.online.
