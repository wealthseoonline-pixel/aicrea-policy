Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: November 23, 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your AICrea account and associated AICrea data. Account deletion is separate from cancelling a Google Play subscription.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open AICrea and sign in with the Google account you use for AICrea.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm permanent deletion.

The in-app deletion request is sent securely to AICrea's account service. If deletion cannot be completed, the app displays an error and you can contact support.

2. If you cannot access the app

Email support@mexbui.online from the email address associated with your AICrea Google account. Write “AICrea account deletion” in the subject. We may ask for limited information to verify that you own the account. Do not send your Google password or payment-card details.

3. Data deleted

When the account deletion request succeeds, AICrea deletes the account and its linked cloud records, including profile data, saved items, conversations, project records, credit-wallet and credit-history records, purchase records held by AICrea, referral and promotion records, support tickets, and stored social-account connection records. AICrea also attempts to revoke connected Meta and Google authorization tokens, removes tracked temporary clip files, and deletes cloud project files stored for your account.

4. Data that may remain outside AICrea

  • Files and projects stored only on your phone are not deleted by cloud account deletion. Remove those from your device separately.
  • Google Play retains its own order and payment records under Google's policies.
  • Deleting your AICrea account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Third-party platforms may retain records under their own policies. You can also remove AICrea's access from the security or connected-app settings of those platforms.
  • Limited information may be retained where required by law or where necessary to address security, fraud or legal claims.

5. Timing

Deletion is processed after you confirm the request and the account service completes the deletion. If a technical error prevents completion, contact support and include the account email and the error shown by the app.

6. Contact and related policies

For help, contact support@mexbui.online.
