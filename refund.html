Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how AICrea handles subscription cancellations, one-time credit purchases and billing support. It does not limit any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

1. Where purchases are processed

Purchases made inside the Android app are processed through Google Play. The applicable Google Play purchase terms and refund rules govern the payment transaction. AICrea does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

2. Subscription cancellation

You can cancel an active subscription in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or by visiting Google Play subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals according to Google Play's rules. It does not automatically refund a charge already made or necessarily end access immediately; access normally continues through the paid period unless the store says otherwise.

3. One-time AI credit purchases

Credit packs are one-time purchases, separate from recurring subscriptions. Credits used for an AI operation that was successfully completed are generally not refundable merely because the output was not preferred. If a purchase was duplicated, unauthorized, not delivered, or a technical failure prevented the purchased credits from being added or used, contact us so we can investigate and help with the appropriate correction or refund route.

4. How to request help with a purchase

Email support@mexbui.online from the email address linked to your AICrea account. Include your Google Play order number, the product name, approximate purchase date, and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords, full card numbers or security codes.

For a refund request that Google Play handles directly, use the refund option in your Google Play order history. AICrea can investigate product-delivery issues and provide relevant purchase details, but cannot guarantee that Google Play will approve a refund.

5. Promotional and free credits

Free, promotional or referral credits have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. Any eligibility rules for a promotion are shown in the app or in the relevant promotion terms.

6. Contact

For billing support, contact support@mexbui.online. Include only the information needed to identify the transaction.
